# 🫁 LCTfound: A Foundational Model for Lung CT Imaging and Analysis

**LCTfound** is a foundational model for lung computed tomography (CT), designed to empower a broad spectrum of clinical tasks with a single, robust, self-supervised learning framework.

This repository provides code for:
- 🔧 **Pretraining** based on diffusion models (completed and available)
- 📊 **Downstream tasks** such as diagnosis, segmentation, and reconstruction (in progress and coming soon)

---

## 📘 About LCTfound

> *“The concomitant development and evolution of lung computed tomography (CT) and artificial intelligence (AI) has allowed non-invasive lung imaging to be a key part of the clinical care of patients with major diseases, such as lung cancer.”*

Despite advances in AI, the lack of large-scale labeled datasets has hindered real-world clinical deployment of AI in lung CT. **LCTfound** addresses this by learning from over **105,000 lung CT scans** (totaling more than **28 million images**) across multiple centers using self-supervised **diffusion-based pretraining**.

---

## 🚀 Highlights

- 🔄 **Self-supervised diffusion-based pretraining**
- 📚 **Multi-task generalization** across 8 categories of lung CT tasks
- 🧠 **Few-shot learning capability** that outperforms previous state-of-the-art models
- 🏥 **Real-world clinical utility**: from high-level diagnosis to low-level imaging enhancement

---

## 🧪 Evaluation Tasks

LCTfound has been evaluated across a diverse set of downstream tasks, including:

1. **Segmentation** of mediastinal neoplasm  
2. **Diagnosis** of pulmonary alveolar proteinosis  
3. **Prognosis** of non-small cell lung cancer  
4. **Prediction** of major pathological response to neoadjuvant chemoimmunotherapy  
5. **3D whole lung modeling** for surgical navigation  
6. **Virtual lung CT angiography (CTA)**  
7. **Sparse-view reconstruction**  
8. **Enhancement** of low-dose CT images
